# ADR-0040: Phase 1 dependencies and reused external functions

Status: accepted (Phase 1)

- **Added in the `models` dependency group** (approved downloads):
  - lightgbm 4.7.0 (MIT);
  - sentence-transformers 6.1.0 (Apache-2.0), pulling torch 2.14.1 (BSD-3-Clause and others) and transformers 5.20.2 (Apache-2.0; I had quoted 5.19.0, and the resolver chose 5.20.2);
  - model weights `sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2` (Apache-2.0, about 470 MB) in `data/cache/hf`, gitignored.
- **Written by hand,** as the brief requires: item-kNN and EASE, affinity, NDCG, the ratio bootstrap, the slate rule and PCA (via `numpy.linalg.svd`).
- **Every reused external function** is listed with its source and licence in `docs/phase1/EXTERNAL_FUNCTIONS.md`. The list is generated by `scripts/phase1_external.py` from the code's AST, and method calls on library objects are attributed by hand.
